Each frame is an iPhone 14 held sideways at 1:1 (844 × 390 CSS pixels). The dashed blue line is the safe area: 47 px clear at each side for the camera notch and 21 px at the bottom for the home bar. Content sits a further 12 px inside it. The pictures behind the interface stand in for the 3D scene; the board will match the blueprint.

Larger screens keep this layout. Panels and type scale with the screen height (×1 at 390 px, up to ×1.6), and the scene fills the rest.

13 · About Gebeta

A reading panel over the home scene, with the title in both scripts.

About Gebeta · ገበጣ

Gebeta is played across Ethiopia. In the 12-hole version there are two rows of six holes, one row for each player.

Children scoop the holes into the earth and play with small pebbles, four in every hole to start.

You sow the stones around the board one by one. Stones are taken in fours, and four stones together are called a ቤት (bet, "home").
