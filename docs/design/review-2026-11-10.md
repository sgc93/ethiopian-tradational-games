# Gebeta: review and scores, 2026-11-10

The owner asked for every element of the game to be scored out of 10, for what would take each to 10, and for that to be built, to the standard of a video game in design, animation, interface and experience, with well designed animation and a 3D environment.

Scores are of the build at commit `5c2af75` (the board alone in a daylight studio, the leather-and-brass interface). They are judged from the code and from captures in headless Chrome's software renderer; sound is judged from the code only, since it cannot be heard here.

## Scores

| # | Element | Score | Why it is not 10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Board and pieces | 8 | The carved board is the best thing in the game. The stones never turn in the air, and their edges and the horns' are stair-stepped on the middle and low tiers. |
| 2 | 3D environment | 2 | There is none: a flat paper backdrop. No place, no depth, nothing alive. |
| 3 | Light and rendering | 6 | Clean daylight and soft shadows, but the light has no character (no sky, no shade, no air) and only the high tier is anti-aliased. |
| 4 | Stone animation | 5 | Every stone slides along the same arc at the same ease. No tumble, no weight on landing, no settle; the hand of stones floats rigidly; a capture is one plain arc. |
| 5 | Camera animation | 6 | Views ease into each other and the play view breathes. There is no opening shot and the result view shows only floor. |
| 6 | Interface animation | 4 | Panels fade in and out. Nothing else moves: no entrance for the bars or the menu, no press feel, no change of turn, scores jump. |
| 7 | Game feel and feedback | 5 | Dust, sparks, pulsing rings and a store glow exist. A capture says nothing ("+4"), a win has no celebration, and nothing shows where a hand of stones will land. |
| 8 | Interface design | 8 | The leather, brass and bone design holds together. Small faults: the result title wraps to three lines; setup answers could read better. |
| 9 | Experience and flow | 6 | The path from home to a result is clear. "Start again" and "Quit" act without asking; a first-time player gets no preview of a move. |
| 10 | Sound | 4 | Synthesised stone and coin sounds only. The fireside ambience went with the night and nothing replaced it; no stings for a capture, a round or a win. |
| 11 | Teaching | 6 | A guided lesson exists and works. Not changed in this pass. |
| 12 | Performance | 7 | Four quality tiers and a 1.6 MB download. Never measured on a real phone. |
| 13 | Languages and access | 7 | English and Amharic throughout, keyboard play, reduced motion. Amharic strings still wait for the owner's reading. |

## What takes each to 10

1. **Board and pieces.** Stones tumble in flight and come to rest at their own angle. Anti-aliasing on every tier with a post chain.
2. **3D environment.** A highland homestead yard in daylight: the board on a woven mat on packed earth, a round thatched house, dry-season grass moving in the wind, eucalyptus and acacia at the yard's edge, blue ridges of mountains under a sky with drifting cloud, leaf shade moving across the mat, dust in the sunlight.
3. **Light and rendering.** The sun as the key light, the sky as the fill and as the wood's reflection, aerial haze for depth, leaf shade as the light's character.
4. **Stone animation.** Stones lift one after another, turn in the air, fall with weight, bounce once and settle; the hand travels in hops; captured stones spiral up and stream to the store one behind another; the deal at a new round is a fountain from the stores.
5. **Camera animation.** An opening crane shot from over the yard down to the board; a low, wide result view with the yard behind the board.
6. **Interface animation.** The bars slide in, the menu deals in one plaque at a time, plaques press, the turn caption changes with a motion, counts and scores tick, the result panel builds in steps.
7. **Game feel and feedback.** A "+4" rises from a capture; a ring snaps on the cup where a hand relays; the cup a move will end its first lap in is marked while the pointer is on a cup; a win throws gold dust from the winner's store.
8. **Interface design.** Fix the result title and the faults the captures show.
9. **Experience and flow.** "Start again" and "Quit" ask once before acting.
10. **Sound.** A daylight ambience (breeze, birds) in place of the fire; a wooden knock for stones in wooden cups; plucked-string stings for a capture, a round and a win.
11. **Teaching, performance, languages.** Need a real phone and the owner's reading more than they need code; listed in the progress record as still open.
